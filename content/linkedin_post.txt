Most AI agents fail at 3 AM because they have amnesia.

When production breaks, standard RAG retrieves text embeddings. But embeddings can't tell you that restarting pods failed 6 weeks ago for that exact error code.

We built IncidentLens to give SRE agents an active feedback loop using Hindsight:

• 4-way recall: BM25, semantic, service graph, and temporal deploy correlation.
• Engineers validate fixes: WORKED vs FAILED.
• Outcomes are retained as experience facts in Hindsight.
• Next outage, it ranks verified fixes and warns against repeating failed steps.
• Resolves cross-service conflicts via reflection.

Result: 100% root cause identification, active avoidance of deadly failed actions, and verified closed-loop negative constraint acquisition on unseeded holdouts.

Code: https://github.com/gr-0x076/incidentlens
